# Help us improve 3D world rendering on very limited hardware – SIMALITÄT

We are building SIMALITÄT, an experimental persistent digital world.

Our goal is ambitious: to simulate and visualize a large, connected world while working with very limited hardware.

Our current development setup includes an NVIDIA GeForce 940M and Godot 4.7.2 using the Compatibility Renderer.

We have already built functioning parts of our world, including buildings and environments. Some individual structures, such as our church, are already looking promising.

However, we want to improve the overall visual quality.

We are looking for developers, graphics programmers, 3D artists and open-source contributors who can help us with:

- Better 3D rendering on older GPUs
- Realistic buildings, terrain and materials
- Efficient lighting and shadows
- Level of Detail and world streaming
- Low-memory rendering techniques
- Procedural environment generation
- Godot and Blender workflows

We are particularly interested in small, practical solutions that can be tested independently and integrated into an existing project.

We do not want to replace our current architecture.

We want to improve it step by step.

Our hardware limitations are part of the experiment. We want to demonstrate how much of a persistent digital world can be achieved with very limited resources.

Our ambition is big. Our hardware is not.

If you have experience in any of these areas, we would appreciate technical advice, repository recommendations, small code examples or collaboration.

Project: SIMALITÄT
